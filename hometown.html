My Hometown

Kota Kinabalu (popularly known as KK) is the bustling, multicultural capital city of Sabah, located on the northwest coast of Malaysian Borneo facing the South China Sea. Serving as the main economic, administrative, and tourism gateway to East Malaysia, the city blends coastal relaxation with rich indigenous heritage and modern urban conveniences.

Founded in 1899 by the British North Borneo Company, the settlement was originally named Jesselton after Sir Charles Jessel, who was the Vice-Chairman of the company at the time.
